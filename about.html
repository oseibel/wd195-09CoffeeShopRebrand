Our Story

The owner of The Rookwood's Roast, Jane Doe, was tired of paying for expensive coffee that wasn't up to her usual standards. One morning, she decided to make her own coffee, having enough of money wasted on companies who focused more on revenue, rather than people. When said coffee was maid, she was delighted by how much better it tasted, pleased from the effort by making something so delicious. A thought then struck... "What if I made this into a business?" Since then, Jane Doe has managed to open 5 different locations around the state, providing the highest quality of beverages to our customers.

What We Believe

We believe that high quality beverages are the most important part of your experience. Every week, we get a fresh shipment of local coffee's and tea's, and only make the fresh drinks after you order them. We are also environmentally concious, using methods that reduce our carbon emissions, as well as not using any plastic. Our to-go cups and straws are made from recycled cardboard and bamboo, giving the same strength as plastic. We also offer non-dairy options, such as almond milk, oat milk, and soy milk. It's important that we get your preferences and order right, so you never have to worry about a wrong coffee order again.

Closing

The journey hasn't been easy, but we pride ourselves in what we have accomplished thus far. We plan on opening more locations, will be more environmentally concious, and will give you the comforting drink of your dreams.
